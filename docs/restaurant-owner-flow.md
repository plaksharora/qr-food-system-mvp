# Restaurant Owner Flow

The restaurant-owner side is focused on smoother operations, customer ownership, and better visibility.

## Flow

1. Owner manages menu items and categories.
2. Owner creates QR codes for tables.
3. Customer places an order directly from the table.
4. Order appears on the order dashboard and kitchen display.
5. Staff accepts, prepares, and marks the order ready.
6. Owner sees direct orders, bookings, customers, and basic analytics.

## Owner Benefits

- Reduced waiter load
- Fewer order mistakes
- Faster service
- Direct customer data
- Direct booking capture
- Higher average order value through upsells
- Better brand consistency
- Less dependency on third-party platforms

## Business Insight

Restaurants do not only need a digital menu. They need a direct digital system that helps them own orders, customer relationships, bookings, and data.
