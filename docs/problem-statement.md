# Problem Statement

Most cafés have shifted to QR menus, but many QR menus are only PDF links.

The customer scans the QR code, opens the menu, zooms in, checks items, and still has to call the waiter to place the order. The restaurant has digitized the menu, but not the dining experience.

## Key Frictions

- QR menus often add browsing friction instead of reducing it.
- Customers still depend on waiters for order placement.
- Orders can still be misheard, repeated incorrectly, or entered wrong.
- PDF menus are difficult to browse on small mobile screens.
- The digital touchpoint often feels generic and disconnected from the café brand.
- Restaurants do not capture useful customer data from the interaction.

## Core Insight

QR menus solved the wrong layer.

They made the menu digital, but they did not make the restaurant experience smarter, smoother, or more brand-owned.
