# Demo Restaurant: Saajh Café

Saajh Café is a fictional premium modern Indian café created for the MVP demo.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, premium, modern Indian, intimate, comfortable, and approachable.

## Brand Meaning

“Saajh” suggests evening, pause, togetherness, warmth, and conversation.

## Visual Direction

- Warm ivory background
- Deep coffee brown text
- Muted terracotta accents
- Soft gold details
- Rounded premium cards
- Calm animations
- Modern Indian café feel
- Premium but approachable design

## Menu Categories

- Signature Coffee
- Cold Brews
- All-Day Plates
- Indian Comfort
- Pasta
- Desserts
- Combos

## Demo Purpose

Saajh Café helps demonstrate how a restaurant’s QR experience can feel like its own branded digital space rather than a generic PDF menu.
