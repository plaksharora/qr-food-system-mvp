# Customer Flow

The customer experience is designed around a simple table QR journey.

## Flow

1. Customer enters the café.
2. Customer scans the QR code placed on the table.
3. The café’s branded ordering page opens.
4. The table number is detected automatically.
5. Customer browses menu categories.
6. Customer views dish photos, prices, descriptions, and badges.
7. Customer customizes an item if needed.
8. Customer adds items to cart.
9. Customer reviews the cart.
10. Customer places the order.
11. Order is sent directly to the restaurant dashboard/kitchen screen.
12. Customer sees order confirmation and basic order status.

## Customer Benefits

- No waiting for a waiter
- Faster ordering
- Fewer communication errors
- Better browsing experience
- Easier food discovery
- More premium café experience

## Experience Principle

The digital experience should feel like the café’s own brand, not like a generic QR menu template.
