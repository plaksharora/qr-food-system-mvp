# Future Scope

Possible future modules for the product:

- Online payments
- Loyalty system
- Personalized offers
- WhatsApp updates
- AI-based upselling
- Inventory management
- POS integration
- Multi-branch dashboard
- Customer retention campaigns
- Table reservations with availability
- Staff role management
- Advanced analytics

## Future Direction

The long-term vision is not only to replace QR menus. The larger opportunity is to help restaurants own their digital dining relationship across ordering, bookings, customer data, loyalty, and repeat visits.

The current MVP focuses only on proving the customer experience and restaurant dashboard workflow.
