# Product Thinking

## Idea

The QR code is not the product. It is only the entry point.

The actual product is a restaurant-owned digital food system where customers can scan a table QR, browse a branded menu experience, customize items, place orders, book tables, and help the restaurant collect direct customer data.

## Product Positioning

A normal QR menu only shows dishes.

A QR food system improves the full dining workflow:

- discovery inside the menu
- order placement
- kitchen visibility
- booking capture
- customer data
- upselling
- basic analytics
- brand consistency

## Why It Matters

Cafés spend heavily on interiors, lighting, furniture, music, packaging, and plating. The digital experience should continue the same brand feeling instead of opening a generic PDF.

RestaurantOS explores how restaurants can own their digital dining relationship instead of depending only on third-party platforms or generic QR menus.
